The Four Noble Truths

The Four Noble Truths are one of the central teachings of Buddhism. They explain the nature of suffering, its cause, its ending, and the path leading toward its ending.

1. The truth of suffering

Life contains experiences that are unsatisfactory or difficult. Buddhism calls this dukkha.

💡 Simple example:
Even when something makes us happy, we know that the experience can change.
