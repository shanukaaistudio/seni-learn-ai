☸ Buddhism · Lesson 1

The Four Noble Truths

The Four Noble Truths are a central framework in Buddhist teaching. They describe the problem of dukkha, its origin, its cessation, and the path leading to its cessation.

💡 Think of it like this

Imagine a doctor. First the doctor identifies the illness, finds its cause, explains that recovery is possible, and then gives a treatment. The Four Noble Truths follow a similar structure.
